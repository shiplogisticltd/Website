1. Agreement to Terms & Corporate Identity

These Terms of Service ("Terms") constitute a legally binding agreement between you (whether an individual, firm, or corporate enterprise, herein "Client", "Consignor", or "Shipper") and Shipmate Logistics Private Limited (CIN: U60232MH2024PTC419820, GSTIN: 27AAGCS1234F1Z5), regarding the booking, carriage, and handling of cargo.

By requesting a freight quote, placing a vehicle indent, issuing a Lorry Receipt (LR) / Consignment Note, or tendering cargo to our network, you agree to be bound by these Terms and the provisions of the Carriage by Road Act, 2007 and rules framed thereunder.

2. Scope of Services

Shipmate provides pan-India road freight forwarding and transportation services, including Full Truckload (FTL), Part Truckload (LTL), Temperature-Controlled Reefer Movement, Over-Dimensional Cargo (ODC) Movement, and Container Drayage. Shipmate reserves the right to select appropriate routes, vehicle types, transshipment hubs, and operational schedules to ensure cargo safety and transit efficiency.

3. Freight Quotations & Spot Bookings

  • Validity: Freight spot quotations provided via website, email, or telephone are valid for 24 hours from issuance unless explicitly stated otherwise in writing.
  • Exclusions: Unless expressly specified in the contract, freight quotes exclude toll charges, green cess, border entry taxes, statutory loading/unloading (hamali) charges, warehousing beyond free transit days, and GST (applicable under Reverse Charge Mechanism or Forward Charge as per agreement).
  • Fuel Surcharge: Rate contracts are subject to fuel price escalation/de-escalation indexation based on prevailing diesel prices in the state of dispatch.

4. Consignor Duties, Packaging & Statutory Compliance

The Consignor warrants and undertakes that:

  • All consignments are packed, crated, strapped, and marked sufficiently to withstand ordinary risks of road transit, vibration, and multi-tier stacking.
  • Every consignment is accompanied by valid statutory commercial documentation, including genuine Tax Invoices, Delivery Challans, and active E-Way Bills (Part A and Part B) matching the vehicle registration number prior to departure.
  • The Consignor shall fully indemnify Shipmate against any penalties, seizures, taxes, or legal costs arising from inaccurate descriptions, undervalued declarations, expired E-Way Bills, or misdeclared weights.
Weight Verification Mandate: Overloading beyond permissible Gross Vehicle Weight (GVW) limits prescribed by the Ministry of Road Transport and Highways (MoRTH) is strictly prohibited. Excess weight detected will be offloaded at Consignor expense.

5. Prohibited, Hazardous & Contraband Cargo

Shipmate strictly refuses carriage of:

  • Explosives, ammunition, fireworks, weapons, and unlicensed radioactive substances.
  • Narcotics, psychotropic drugs, contraband, liquor (without state transport permits), and wildlife derivatives.
  • Currency notes, bullion, precious metals, and negotiable bearer instruments.
  • Hazardous chemicals without Prior Material Safety Data Sheets (MSDS), TREM cards, and emergency response approvals.

6. Loading, Unloading & Detention Charges

Standard free loading and unloading time allowance is 24 hours each at origin and destination for FTL consignments, and 2 hours for LTL consignments.

Any detention beyond the free window due to customer dock unavailability, consignee refusal, or documentation delays shall attract vehicle detention charges at our standard daily slab rates until release.

7. Payment Terms, Billing & Carrier's Lien

  • Freight charges must be remitted strictly as per agreed payment terms (e.g., To-Pay on delivery, Advance against loading, or agreed credit tenure).
  • Delayed payments beyond credit terms shall attract interest at 18% per annum from the due date.
  • Carrier's Particular & General Lien: Shipmate Logistics holds a right of general and particular lien over goods in its possession for all freight, demurrage, and storage charges due from the Consignor or Consignee.

8. Limitation of Liability & Transit Insurance

All cargo is accepted at Owner's Risk unless specifically booked under "Carrier's Risk" with transit insurance premium paid in advance. Consignors are strongly advised to obtain comprehensive Marine Cargo Insurance coverage for full invoice value.

Under Section 10 of the Carriage by Road Act, 2007, Shipmate's maximum liability for cargo damage or shortage shall not exceed the statutory limits or the freight value of the damaged portion, whichever is lower. Shipmate shall not be liable for losses caused by Force Majeure events, natural disasters, state-wide bandhs, riots, or civil unrest.

9. Governing Law & Jurisdiction

These Terms and any transport contracts entered into shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the Republic of India. In the event of any legal dispute or claim arising under or in connection with our services, the competent courts located in Raigad / Mumbai, Maharashtra shall have exclusive jurisdiction.
