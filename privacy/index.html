1. Overview & Corporate Identification

This Privacy Policy sets forth the privacy practices of Shipmate Logistics Private Limited (referred to as "Shipmate", "Company", "we", "us", or "our"), incorporated under the Companies Act, 2013, with Corporate Identification Number (CIN) U52290MH2025PTC453719 and Goods and Services Tax Identification Number (GSTIN) 27AAGCS1234F1Z5.

We operate the website https://shipmatelogistics.in and provide pan-India logistics, road freight, Full Truckload (FTL), Part Truckload (LTL), Cold Chain, and Containerized transportation solutions. We respect your privacy and are committed to safeguarding your personal and commercial data in accordance with the Information Technology Act, 2000, and the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act).

2. Information We Collect

We collect information only when actively and voluntarily provided by you through our freight quote calculators, contact forms, account registration, or direct communications with our dispatch desk:

  • Identity & Contact Details: Name, company/business entity, billing address, official email address, mobile number (+91 77278 06089 / +91 73571 77827), and designated contact person.
  • Freight & Consignment Data: Pickup and drop locations, pincodes, cargo classification, gross weight, dimensions, packaging requirements, and statutory E-Way Bill / GST credentials needed for road transportation.
  • Payment & Billing Safeguards: We do not collect, receive, process, or store sensitive financial credentials such as credit/debit card numbers, CVVs, net-banking passwords, or UPI PINs on our servers. All digital transactions and freight advances are processed securely through RBI-compliant banking networks or PCI-DSS certified payment interfaces.
  • Contact Form Transmission: Inquiries submitted through our online quotation forms are routed directly to our authorized dispatch desk for rate estimation and route planning. Form data is never stored on public-facing databases or monetized.
  • Technical & Telematics Data: Standard server logs (IP address, browser type, device operating system, access timestamps) captured automatically by hosting infrastructure for cyber security, operational uptime, and fraud prevention.

3. How We Use Your Information

All information collected is processed strictly for legitimate commercial and operational purposes, including:

  • Generating freight estimations, route feasibilities, and customized rate contracts.
  • Orchestrating dispatch, real-time GPS tracking, and delivery confirmation for assigned shipments.
  • Complying with statutory transportation guidelines, including GST e-Invoicing, E-Way Bill generation, and the Carriage by Road Act, 2007.
  • Facilitating client customer service, incident resolution, and account administration.
  • Preventing fraudulent bookings and unauthorized access to fleet dispatch portals.
Important Note on Commercial Data: Shipmate Logistics does not sell, lease, or monetize customer shipment histories, freight rates, or consignor/consignee records to third-party data brokers or advertisers under any circumstances.

4. DPDP Act & Statutory Compliance

In alignment with India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act (DPDP Act, 2023), Shipmate processes personal data strictly on lawful grounds—namely your affirmative consent when requesting services, or for legitimate uses such as executing transport agreements and fulfilling statutory mandates.

You have the right to request access to the personal data we hold about you, request rectification of inaccurate records, or withdraw your consent for discretionary communications by contacting our Compliance Desk.

5. Information Sharing and Disclosure

We may share your consignment and contact data solely under the following conditions:

  • Authorized Logistics Partners: Verified fleet drivers, regional hub handlers, and loading supervisors strictly to the extent required to execute loading, transit, and handover.
  • Regulatory & Law Enforcement Bodies: Commercial tax departments, state border check-posts, transport authorities, or judicial agencies when mandated by law or court order.
  • Technical Infrastructure Providers: Secure cloud hosting, SMS/WhatsApp notification gateways, and mapping telematics providers bound by strict confidentiality agreements.

6. Data Security and Retention

We implement industry-standard administrative, physical, and digital security measures to safeguard all digital information against unauthorized access, loss, or alteration. All electronic form submissions are transmitted using Transport Layer Security (TLS 1.3 / SSL encryption).

Consignment documentation (consignment notes, LR copies, proof of delivery) is retained for the statutory period required under Indian taxation and transport laws (typically 7 to 8 financial years).

7. Cookies and Analytics

Our website uses standard essential cookies and lightweight performance metrics to ensure seamless navigation, remember route calculator parameters, and analyze website speed. You can configure your browser to decline non-essential cookies without affecting basic quote request functionality.
